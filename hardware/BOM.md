## Power

- **A. USB along the hinge** — best
- **B. Clip-off LiPo** — charge *outside* the fridge, never at 4 °C
- **C. Power bank on top of fridge** — rental-friendly

Inner liner is plastic. Magnets will not hold the camera inside.
