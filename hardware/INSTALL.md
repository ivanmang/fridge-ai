# FridgeSnap — 10 minute install

Clip the puck onto a **door bottle-shelf**. Lens looks into the shelves when the door is 30–45° open. No screws.

1. Flash firmware and join Wi-Fi on the table. Open `http://fridgesnap.local/`.
2. Clean the plastic with alcohol.
3. Clip onto the mid door bin, lens inward and slightly down.
4. Check the puck will not smash a shelf when the door shuts.
5. Tape the reed switch on the puck; magnet on the steel cabinet so it closes with the door.
6. Run thin USB-C down the **hinge** fold, not across the main gasket.
7. Close the door 10 times and confirm a new photo each time.

A sealed box fogs. Vent the case. Charge lithium packs outside the fridge.
